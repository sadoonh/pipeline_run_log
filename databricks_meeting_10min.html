Meeting brief · Data engineering

RDS → Databricks

Architecture, developer permissions, and ownership — prioritized for a short alignment meeting.

10 MIN TOTAL
Opening statement: “We want to ingest our AWS RDS PostgreSQL data into Databricks and give our analytics team enough self-service access to build, run, and monitor pipelines — without needing admin privileges.”

01 / Ask these questions first

~6 MIN
01What is the approved RDS ingestion approach?

Native connector / CDC, scheduled JDBC loads, DMS, or an existing internal pattern? Can it capture updates and deletes?

02Is RDS ↔ Databricks connectivity already available?

Network path, security groups, credentials, and cross-account access. If not, who provisions it?

03Can our team get its own development schema?

Unity Catalog access to create and modify our own tables/views, plus read access to approved sources.

04Can we use compute without repeated tickets?

Notebooks, SQL warehouses, Python/Spark, and approved compute policies or shared resources.

05Can we own jobs and pipelines end-to-end?

Create, schedule, monitor, rerun, and troubleshoot jobs. Can a service principal own production runs?

06What's the exact access-request process?

Required groups, tickets, approvers, and a standard developer role/profile if available.

~2 MIN

Build & executeNotebooks; Python/Spark compute; SQL warehouse; jobs and pipelines.
Operate & deployJob logs/monitoring; secrets; service principal; Git/CI/CD integration.

03 / Confirm before leaving

~2 MIN
Meeting notes (optional)
